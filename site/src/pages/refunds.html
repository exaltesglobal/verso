Cancellation and refunds

Cancel any time.

Verso's Free plan lets you try everything, including 3 reels a month with Pro features, before you pay. So paid plans work simply: you can cancel whenever you like, and payments are not refunded.

Effective {{co.effective}}
In short
  • Cancel any time, in the app or at your account page. No questions, no fees.
  • After cancelling, you keep Pro or Team until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Payments already made are not refunded, including for unused time.
  • If you were charged twice, or paid but your plan never activated, you get a full refund.

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers Verso Pro and Verso Team subscriptions, monthly or yearly, bought from {{co.legal_name}} ("we") on verso.video or inside the Verso app. The Free plan costs nothing and has nothing to refund.

2. Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel at any time:

  • in the Verso app, from your account and plan settings; or
  • by signing in to your account page at api.verso.video/account; or
  • by emailing {{co.support_email}} from your account email.

Cancelling stops the next automatic renewal. Your paid plan stays active until the end of the current monthly or yearly period, then your account moves to the Free plan. Your projects, exports and brand kits stay on your computer and remain yours.

3. Refunds

Because you can try Pro features on the Free plan before buying, subscription payments are non-refundable. This includes partially used months or years, and renewals you forgot to cancel. We don't offer pro-rated refunds when you cancel or move to a lower plan.

We will always give a full refund in these cases:

  • Duplicate charge: you were charged more than once for the same period.
  • Failed activation: your payment went through but your plan was not activated, and we couldn't fix it within 7 days of you telling us.
  • Charged after cancelling: you were renewed after you had cancelled.
  • Unauthorised payment: a payment you did not make, reported within 30 days.

Where the law of India gives you a right to a refund beyond this policy, that right applies.

4. How to ask for a refund

Email {{co.support_email}} within 30 days of the charge, from the email on your Verso account, with the Razorpay payment ID (it starts with pay_) and a line on what happened. We reply within 2 working days.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (card, UPI, net banking or wallet) through Razorpay within 5 to 7 working days. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

5. Price changes

If we change the price of a plan, we'll tell you by email at least 30 days before it affects you. Members keep the price they signed up at for as long as their subscription stays active. If you don't want the new price, you can cancel before it applies.
