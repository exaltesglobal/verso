1. Who we are

Verso is made by Agape Labs, a brand of {{co.legal_name}} ("we", "us"), {{co.address}}. We are the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described here. This policy covers the website verso.video, the waitlist, your Verso account (api.verso.video) and the Verso desktop app.

2. What stays on your computer

Verso is built to work on-device. The following are processed only on your own computer and are never sent to us:

  • videos, audio, images and other media you import;
  • transcripts, captions, translations and dubbing;
  • faces, voices and speakers detected for reframing and clean-up;
  • your projects, edits, exports, templates and brand kits.

The AI models Verso uses are downloaded to your computer and run there. We don't use your content to train any model.

3. What we collect, and why

Waitlist

Your email address, and optionally what you make, which computer you use and your language choice. We use these to send your invite and launch news, and to plan beta batches. To stop spam, we briefly use your IP address to limit sign-ups; it is not stored with your entry.

Your account

Your name, email address, and either a password (stored only as a secure hash) or your Google account ID if you sign in with Google. We use these to create your account, sign you in and contact you about it.

Plan and devices

Your plan, invite code, subscription status, and for each computer you sign in on: a random device ID, a device name, the app version and the last time it checked in. The app checks your plan about once a week; this is how Verso works offline while keeping paid features with paying members.

Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay Software Private Limited. Razorpay collects your payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive only the payment and subscription IDs, amount, status, payment method type and the last digits Razorpay shares, which we keep for invoices, GST and support.

Support and email

The messages you send us, and the emails we send you (sign-in links, receipts, beta and product news). You can unsubscribe from news at any time; account and billing emails continue while you have an account.

Crash reports

If Verso crashes, the error details are saved on your computer. They are sent to us only if you choose to send them, and they contain technical information about the error, the app version and your system, not your media.

Website

verso.video uses no analytics or advertising cookies. Our web host keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for security, for a short period. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when you visit.

4. Our legal basis

We process personal data with your consent, which you give when you join the waitlist, create an account or send a crash report, and for the legitimate uses the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 allows, such as providing the service you asked for and meeting our legal obligations. You can withdraw consent at any time; this doesn't affect processing already done.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent personal data, and we don't share it for advertising. We share only what's needed with service providers who process it for us under contract:

  • Hostinger: hosting for verso.video, our servers, database and email;
  • Razorpay: payments and subscriptions;
  • Google: only if you choose Sign in with Google;
  • Surecast: only if you use Send to Surecast, and only the clips you choose to send.

We may also disclose data when Indian law requires it, for example to tax authorities or under a lawful order. Some providers may store data on servers outside India, in countries the law allows.

6. How long we keep it

  • Waitlist entries: until launch plus 12 months, or until you ask us to remove you.
  • Account data: while your account exists. When you delete your account, we erase it within 30 days.
  • Payment and invoice records: 8 years, as required by Indian tax and company law.
  • Server logs: up to 90 days.

7. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • get a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • have it erased, unless we must keep it by law;
  • withdraw your consent;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to;
  • raise a grievance with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India.

Write to {{co.privacy_email}} from your account email. We reply within 30 days.

8. Children

If you are under 18, a parent or legal guardian must create the account and agree to this policy for you. We don't knowingly collect data from children without that consent, track children or show them targeted ads. If you think a child has signed up without consent, write to us and we'll delete the account.

9. Security

We use HTTPS everywhere, store passwords only as strong hashes, keep secrets out of our code and limit who on our team can access account data. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.
