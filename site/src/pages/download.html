Questions

Why is it invite only?

We're opening the beta in batches so we can help every new creator properly and fix issues quickly. Your place on the list sets your batch.

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes. Unlimited editing, captions and 1080p exports with no watermark, plus 3 reels a month with Pro features to try them out. No card needed.

Do my videos get uploaded?

No. Cutting, reframing, captions and clean-up all run on your computer. Only your sign-in and plan details go online.

Can I use it without internet?

Yes. Verso needs internet to sign in, and checks your plan once a week. Everything else works offline.
